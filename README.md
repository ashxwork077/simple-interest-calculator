# Simple Interest Calculator

## Overview
The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple application designed to calculate the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest is calculated using the following formula:

**SI = (P × R × T) / 100**

Where:
- P = Principal Amount
- R = Annual Rate of Interest
- T = Time Period (in years)

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest**

## Example

Suppose:
- Principal = 10,000
- Rate of Interest = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = 1,000

Total Amount = 10,000 + 1,000 = 11,000
